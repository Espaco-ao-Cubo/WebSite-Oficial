# TejoOne Solar Panels First Test

The TejoOne project reached another key milestone: the first functional test of its solar panels and onboard power management system.

## Test Objectives

The main goals were:

- Verify the panels' energy conversion efficiency  
- Test the battery charging regulation system  
- Measure performance under different light conditions  
- Validate communication between the BMS and the EPS

## Results

The outcomes were very promising:

1. **Average efficiency of 22%** — within design specs  
2. **Stable voltage regulation** — less than 2% variation  
3. **Full charge achieved in 3 hours of solar simulation**  
4. **Successful integration with telemetry system**

### Technical Data

```
Nominal voltage: 8.4 V
Maximum current: 2.1 A
Efficiency: 22%
Total area: 0.12 m²
```

## Next Steps

- Thermal resistance testing of the panels  
- Final integration with the CubeSat power system  
- Vacuum thermal chamber qualification tests
