# NewSpace Winter School

**Date:** February 10-14, 2025

The NewSpace Winter School is an intensive one-week program dedicated to satellite development and space technologies.

## About the Program

Over 5 days, participants will have the opportunity to connect with various names from the Portuguese space industry and academia, participating in 5 curricular units covering the entire space sector, through lectures and workshops. At the end of our winter school, there will be a hackathon for you to test your new knowledge!
