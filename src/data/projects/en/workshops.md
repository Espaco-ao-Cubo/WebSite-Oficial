# Educational Workshops

An opportunity to learn about SPACE with university students who are building a satellite!

Espaço ao Cubo proposes interactive workshops, taught by university students, to stimulate students' interest in the space sector. In addition to enriching the school curriculum with a dynamic and engaging activity, your school will be contributing to the development of a CubeSat, a nano-satellite that represents an advancement in space education in Portugal.

Who knows if in the future, the students participating in this Workshop will join us at the forefront of the next generation of satellites?!

## Target Audience

Middle and High School students interested in exploring the fascinating world of space technology.

## Available Workshops

### Workshop 1: The Business of Space

**Duration:** 2h | **Cost:** 4€/student

Space is no longer just for astronauts! This interactive *workshop* explores how the space economy is growing and opening opportunities for innovative companies – from satellites to space tourism. With dynamic activities, students will create their own space business ideas and discover how to transform technology into profitable opportunities. The future of space could be your **next big investment!**

**Program:**

1. Welcome and Introduction
2. Space Economy Overview
3. How Space Companies Make Money
4. Interactive Activity
5. The Role of CubeSats in Space Economy
6. Space Entrepreneurship and Funding
7. Interactive Activity - Pitch
8. Q\&A

***

### Workshop 2: Satellite Systems Engineering

**Duration:** 2h | **Cost:** 4€/student

Satellites are complex systems, composed of various subsystems that require specialization. However, technological innovation requires a global vision capable of integrating and balancing all the pieces – this is where Systems Engineers come in! In this workshop, students put on the engineer's hat and face the challenge of **designing a satellite**, balancing the needs of each subsystem to create a solution. Will they get their project approved?

**Program:**

1. Welcome and Introduction
2. Spacecraft Overview
3. Interactive Activity - Brainstorming sat systems
4. Guide through the different systems
5. Challenge: Project a CubeSat!
6. Q\&A

***

### Workshop 3: Building a CubeSat

**Duration:** 3h30 | **Cost:** 5€/student

In this practical workshop, students will **build a *CubeSat* from scratch**, exploring its main subsystems. Divided into teams, they will work with **electronics, programming, and 3D modeling** using real components, from cameras to attitude control systems. They will have the opportunity to get hands-on, learn new skills, and explore, in a simplified way, the fascinating world of space engineering!

**Program:**

1. Welcome and Introduction
2. CubeSat Overview
3. Subsystems Introduction
4. Hands-On!
   * a. Payload Camera
   * b. Power Management
   * c. Attitude Control
   * d. Antenna
   * e. Structure (CAD)
5. Sharing the Results
6. Q\&A

## Objectives

* Introduce space engineering concepts and sector economics
* Provide practical and interactive experiences
* Foster interest in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics)
* Create a community of students passionate about space
* Contribute to the development of a Portuguese CubeSat
